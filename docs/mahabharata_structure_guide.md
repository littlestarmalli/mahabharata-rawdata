# Mahabharata Structure: Three Counts Explained

## The Simple Answer

The Mahabharata's structure is described using **three different counting systems**, each correct for its own purpose:

| Count | What | Who / Source |
|------:|------|-------------|
| **100** | Canonical sub-parvas (upa-parvas) | Souti/Vaishampayana says this in the Sanskrit text itself |
| **95** | Sub-parvas in BORI Critical Edition | Modern scholars at BORI (Pune) who edited the CE in 1966–1971 |
| **435** | Named section headers in BORI ITRANS files | The typesetting structure used in the digitized Sanskrit files |

They all describe the **same 18 main parvas** — just at different levels of granularity.

> **Why do Souti's numbers differ from BORI?**
> Souti is describing the *vulgate* (traditional) text of ~82,000 shlokas.
> The BORI Critical Edition (1966–1971) removed later interpolations and has only ~73,784 shlokas.
> Both are legitimate — they are different recensions.

## Layer 2 — What Souti Said: The 100 Canonical Sub-Parvas

In **Adi Parva, Chapter 2** (parvasa.ngrahaparva), Souti recites the names of **100 sub-parvas** that make up the 18 main parvas (shlokas 34–69).  
He also says Vyasa originally told 100 parvas, which Lomaharshana then re-organized as 18.

> **Plus 2 appendices** (harivamsha + bhaviShyat) = 102 total, but traditionally called "100 + appendix".

### Example: Why Bhishma Parva has 13 (not 4)

Souti says Bhishma Parva has **4 sub-parvas**: Jambukhanda, Bhumi, Bhagavad-gita, Bhishma-vadha.

| ITRANS Name | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| `prathamayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 1 |
| `dvitIyayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 2 |
| `tRRitIyayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 3 |
| `chaturthayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 4 |
| `pa~nchamayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 5 |
| `ShaShThayuddhadivasaH` | Battle Day 6 |

So: 3 named parva-headers + 10 battle-day headers = **13 total sections** for Bhishma Parva.

## Summary: Which Count Should I Use?

| Purpose | Use |
|---------|-----|
| Citing the traditional/classical tradition | **100** (Souti's list) |
| Citing the modern critical scholarly edition | **95** (BORI CE) |
| Working with the raw Sanskrit digitized text | **435** (ITRANS section headers) |
| Citing the 18 main parvas | **18** (everybody agrees) |

The 100, 95, and 435 are **all internally consistent** — they just describe the same text at different levels of granularity.
